About Drip or Drown

Smart irrigation. Professional service. Healthy landscapes.

Meet Abiti Daba

I am Abiti Daba, founder of Drip or Drown Irrigation and a Bachelor of Management student at the University of Lethbridge.

My interest in business, technology, project management and customer service led me to create Drip or Drown as a practical business focused on professional irrigation solutions.

The company provides irrigation installation, repairs, maintenance, drip irrigation and smart controller upgrades for residential and landscaping applications.

My goal is to combine strong business practices with practical technology and quality workmanship to create irrigation systems that are efficient, reliable and suited to Alberta's climate.
