WHO WE ARE

Irrigation Done Right

Drip or Drown Irrigation is a local irrigation company serving Calgary and surrounding communities. We provide practical irrigation solutions for residential landscapes, gardens and other properties.

Our approach is simple: understand the property, design the system around its watering requirements, and install reliable equipment that provides consistent coverage.

We also provide seasonal services, repairs, upgrades and smart irrigation technology to help property owners maintain healthy landscapes while using water efficiently.

Whether you need a new irrigation system or help improving an existing one, our goal is to provide straightforward service, quality workmanship and dependable support.
