SMART WATER MANAGEMENT

Climate Controlled Irrigation

Weather-aware irrigation can adjust watering based on rainfall and changing outdoor conditions instead of running on a fixed schedule.

How it works

Weather or rain sensors provide information to the irrigation controller. If rain is detected—or other programmed conditions are met—the controller can skip, delay or adjust an irrigation cycle.

ET-based systems use evapotranspiration data such as temperature, wind, humidity and solar conditions to estimate how much water the landscape needs. The controller can then fine-tune watering to help reduce unnecessary water use.
