The cost of an irrigation system depends on factors such as property size, number of zones, landscaping, water source and the type of equipment required. The best way to determine an accurate price is to assess the property and develop a system based on its specific requirements.

Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the property. Smaller residential systems may be completed relatively quickly, while larger or more complex projects can require additional time for trenching, installation, connections and testing.

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the soil around plants through small emitters or dripline. It is particularly useful for gardens, flower beds, planters and other areas where targeted watering is beneficial.

Yes. Smart irrigation controllers can allow homeowners to manage schedules and monitor their irrigation system using a smartphone, depending on the controller and setup.

In Calgary and other Alberta communities, winter temperatures can cause water remaining in irrigation components to freeze and potentially damage the system. Proper winterization helps prepare the system for freezing conditions.

A spring startup generally includes activating the system, checking individual zones, inspecting sprinkler heads and components, identifying leaks or problems, and programming the controller for the upcoming season.

Irrigation systems benefit from regular inspection, especially at the beginning and end of the season. Checking sprinkler heads, valves, connections and controller settings can help identify problems before they become larger issues.
